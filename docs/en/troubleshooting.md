# Troubleshooting

- **Room doesn't heat although it's cold** – check the status sensor: *Heating pause* → use *Heat today* or mode *Always*; *Lowered* → check residents/zone or turn up (suspends for 3 h); *Window open* → check the contact.
- **Valve permanently open (TRV602Z)** – preset "on" means valve open; "On preset" should be *Automatic* or *eco*.
- **Absence detected wrongly (e.g. VPN)** – impossible jumps and inaccurate fixes are ignored; any phone in the zone counts. Inspect the `persons` attribute; use the *Guests* switch for visitors.
- **"Thermostat not responding"** – radio/VPN/battery; Raumklima keeps retrying every 5 min.
- **Cards look outdated** – reload; in the app: reset frontend cache.
- **Settings read-only** – only administrators can edit.
- **Bug report** – download diagnostics (Settings → Devices & services → Raumklima → ⋮) and open an issue.
